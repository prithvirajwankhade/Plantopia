From Tiny Seed to Beautiful Plant

Six illustrative stages of growth

These stages are illustrative. Actual growth varies with plant species, climate and growing conditions.
🌰

Day 1: Planting Stage

Seed is placed in moist soil.

5%
  • Care: Water gently after sowing.
  • Sunlight: Light shade
  • Monitor: Soil stays moist, not soggy
🌱

Day 3: Initial Development

Seed swells and the root starts.

15%
  • Care: Keep soil damp.
  • Sunlight: Bright indirect light
  • Monitor: Check for fungus or ants
🌿

Day 7: Early Growth

First tiny leaves appear.

30%
  • Care: Light watering daily.
  • Sunlight: 3-4 hours of light
  • Monitor: Leans toward light? Turn pot
🪴

Day 15: Growing Stage

More leaves and a stronger stem.

50%
  • Care: Water when top soil is dry.
  • Sunlight: 4-6 hours of light
  • Monitor: Look for pests under leaves
🌳

Day 30: Established Plant

Strong roots and many leaves.

75%
  • Care: Regular watering; start light feeding.
  • Sunlight: Plant's full light need
  • Monitor: Watch leaf colour
🌸

Day 60: Mature Growth

Full size; may flower or fruit.

100%
  • Care: Water by plant type.
  • Sunlight: Full light
  • Monitor: Prune and repot if crowded
