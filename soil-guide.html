Soil and Fertilizer Guide

Good soil means a happy plant.

Soil types compared

Soil comparison
Soil TypeCharacteristicsAdvantagesSuitable Plants
Sandy SoilGritty, drains fast, holds little waterGood drainage and airAloe vera, cactus, carrots
Clay SoilSticky, holds water, drains slowlyRich in nutrientsRice, some shrubs (after adding compost)
Loamy SoilMix of sand, silt and clay; crumblyBest balance of water and airTomato, rose, tulsi, sunflower
Silt SoilSmooth and soft, holds moistureFertile and easy to digVegetables, leafy plants
Potting MixReady-made light mix with compost, coco peat or perliteClean, light, good for potsMoney plant, snake plant, spider plant

Key ideas

  • Drainage: how fast extra water leaves the pot.
  • Aeration: air space around roots.
  • Moisture: how long soil stays damp.
  • Soil pH: acidity from 0 to 14. Most plants like 6-7.
  • Nutrients: mainly nitrogen (leaves), phosphorus (roots, flowers), potassium (overall health).

Natural food for plants

  • Compost: rotted kitchen and garden waste.
  • Organic fertilizers: cow dung manure, bone meal.
  • Vermicompost: compost made by earthworms.
  • Soil amendments: sand or perlite for drainage, coco peat for moisture.

Choose the Right Soil for Your Plant

  1. Does your plant store water (aloe, cactus)? Use sandy, gritty mix.
  2. Is it a houseplant in a pot? Use potting mix with perlite.
  3. Is it a vegetable or flower in the ground? Use loamy soil with compost.
  4. Is your soil heavy clay? Mix in compost and sand.
