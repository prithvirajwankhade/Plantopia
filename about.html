About PLANTOPIA

Grow Your Knowledge. Grow Your Garden.

Project introduction

PLANTOPIA is a beginner gardening website that makes gardening easier and more accessible. It was built for the First Year B.Tech CSE Computer Workshop-I project.

Objectives

  • Teach basic plant care.
  • Help solve common plant problems.
  • Explain soil and fertilizer.
  • Practise HTML, CSS and Bootstrap.

Features

  • Plant encyclopedia with 10 plants
  • Rule-based Plant Doctor
  • Growth timeline
  • Soil guide with comparison table
  • Garden dashboard with XP and badges
