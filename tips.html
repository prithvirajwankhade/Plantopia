Little Gardening Secrets

Simple tips for beginners

💧
Water correctly

Check the soil with your finger. Water deeply, only when the top is dry.

🏺
Choose a pot

Pick a pot with drain holes, 2-3 cm wider than the root ball.

☀️
Give enough sunlight

Match the plant: bright, indirect or full sun.

🍂
Spot unhealthy leaves

Yellow, spotted or curled leaves signal a problem. See Plant Doctor.

🪣
Prepare potting soil

Mix garden soil, compost and sand in equal parts.

♻️
Use compost

Spread a thin layer on top soil every month or two.

✂️
Prune plants

Cut dead or crowded stems with clean scissors.

🐛
Prevent pests

Check under leaves weekly and keep plants clean.

🔄
Repot plants

Repot when roots come out of the holes, ideally in spring.
