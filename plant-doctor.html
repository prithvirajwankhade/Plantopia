Your Plant Looks Unhappy? Let's Help!

Click a problem to open its solution.

This is a rule-based Plant Doctor with fixed advice. It is not an AI diagnosis tool, but can be upgraded in the future.
Yellow Leaves

How to recognise it: Leaves turn pale or yellow, often from the bottom.

Possible causes: Overwatering, poor drainage, low light, nutrient shortage.

Step-by-step treatment:
  1. Check soil moisture
  2. Let the top soil dry
  3. Make sure the pot has drain holes
  4. Remove yellow leaves
  5. Feed lightly if soil is old

Prevention: Water only when soil is dry; use a well-draining mix.

Safe home remedy: Mix a little compost into the topsoil.

Ask an expert when: Most leaves yellow within weeks.

Brown Leaf Tips

How to recognise it: Tips and edges turn dry and brown.

Possible causes: Dry air, salty tap water, irregular watering, too much fertilizer.

Step-by-step treatment:
  1. Trim brown tips with clean scissors
  2. Water evenly
  3. Flush soil with plain water
  4. Use rested or filtered water

Prevention: Keep a regular watering routine and avoid heavy feeding.

Safe home remedy: Leave tap water in an open jug overnight.

Ask an expert when: Browning keeps spreading after fixes.

Wilting

How to recognise it: Stems and leaves droop and look limp.

Possible causes: Underwatering, overwatering, heat stress, root damage.

Step-by-step treatment:
  1. Touch the soil: dry or soggy?
  2. If dry, water deeply
  3. If soggy, stop watering and let it dry
  4. Move out of harsh afternoon sun

Prevention: Check soil every 2-3 days in summer.

Safe home remedy: Move to light shade during heat waves.

Ask an expert when: Plant stays limp after 2-3 days.

Slow Growth

How to recognise it: Few or no new leaves for weeks.

Possible causes: Low light, small pot, poor soil, no nutrients, cold season.

Step-by-step treatment:
  1. Move to brighter place
  2. Check if roots fill the pot
  3. Repot into fresh mix
  4. Feed lightly in growing season

Prevention: Repot every 1-2 years and give enough light.

Safe home remedy: Add compost or vermicompost.

Ask an expert when: No growth for months in good season.

Falling Leaves

How to recognise it: Healthy leaves drop suddenly.

Possible causes: Sudden change of place, drafts, over or underwatering, low light.

Step-by-step treatment:
  1. Keep the plant in one spot
  2. Avoid cold or hot drafts
  3. Fix the watering routine
  4. Remove fallen leaves

Prevention: Change the plant's location slowly.

Safe home remedy: None needed; fix the cause.

Ask an expert when: Heavy leaf drop continues.

Pest Infestation

How to recognise it: Tiny insects, sticky leaves, webbing or holes.

Possible causes: Aphids, mealybugs, spider mites, crowding, dry air.

Step-by-step treatment:
  1. Isolate the plant
  2. Wipe leaves with a damp cloth
  3. Spray mild soap water
  4. Repeat every 3-4 days
  5. Check under leaves

Prevention: Inspect plants weekly and keep leaves clean.

Safe home remedy: Mild soap solution (a few drops in 1 litre water) or neem oil spray.

Ask an expert when: Pests return after 2-3 rounds.

Root Rot

How to recognise it: Soft, black, smelly roots; yellow wilting leaves.

Possible causes: Constant wet soil, no drainage holes, heavy soil.

Step-by-step treatment:
  1. Take the plant out of the pot
  2. Cut off black or mushy roots
  3. Dry roots for an hour
  4. Repot in fresh dry mix
  5. Water lightly

Prevention: Always use pots with holes and well-draining soil.

Safe home remedy: Add sand or perlite to the mix.

Ask an expert when: Most roots are already mushy.

Fungal Infection

How to recognise it: Spots, white powder or fuzzy patches on leaves.

Possible causes: High humidity, wet leaves, poor air flow, crowding.

Step-by-step treatment:
  1. Remove affected leaves
  2. Water at the soil, not the leaves
  3. Improve air flow
  4. Keep the plant dry in the evening

Prevention: Space plants apart and avoid wet leaves.

Safe home remedy: Baking soda spray (1 tsp in 1 litre water) on mild cases.

Ask an expert when: Spots spread quickly to many leaves.
